  A 4 × 224 Gb/s Single-Ended PAM-4 Transceiver
  Front-End With Noise Suppression Technique and
   Cascaded Equalizers in 130-nm SiGe BiCMOS
Xiongshi Luo , Graduate Student Member, IEEE, Xuewei You, Zhenghao Li , Graduate Student Member, IEEE,

   Abstract—A dc-coupled analog single-ended (SE) transceiver
(TRX) front-end supporting 224 Gb/s/lane is presented. It
features SE-to-differential (S2D) and differential-to-SE (D2S)
conversion, power-efficient broadband analog equalization, and
noise suppression. Both the transmitter and receiver front-ends
adopt pseudodifferential structures with dual-loop regulators to
achieve a high power supply rejection ratio (PSRR). A power
bounce canceller (PBC) is proposed for the S2D to reduce the
power bounce noise by 76% and improve the eye width (EW)
by 12%. Moreover, cascaded continuous-time linear equalizers
(CTLEs) with emitter degradation, distributed-peaking tech-
nique, and a feedforward low-pass branch are employed to enable
low-/medium-/high-frequency equalization, supporting up to
29 dB loss compensation. Fabricated in 130-nm SiGe BiCMOS,
the proposed SE TRX front-end demonstrates a data rate of
224 Gb/s PAM-4 with an energy efficiency of 2.08 pJ/b and an
FoM of 0.07 pJ/b/dB. To the best of the author’s knowledge, this                      Fig. 1. (a) Conventional LR link and (b) proposed LR link based on SR
